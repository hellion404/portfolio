Poo Man’s Love

Medium
Wood, deconstructed troll doll, VHS reel and tape (from Ice Age), and acrylic paint
Size
14″ × 11″ × 11″
Year
2026

Poo Man’s Love depicts a coiled creature with its arms reaching outward for its embrace. The face and arms come from a troll doll I took apart, and the halo is a reel from a VHS copy of the film Ice Age. I kinda just jumped straight into this one without much thought. I figured it would be funny to make, so I made it.

I believe there are two ways of looking at this piece. The first is that it depicts some sort of a false angel: a serpent with a toy’s grin, arms open for a hug, wearing a halo made from a kids’ movie—or like an idol, something to set on a table and look up to. When I look at it through this interpretation, I think about how much we let culture shape us and how quickly that culture turns over. A troll doll and a VHS tape were both everywhere once. Now they’re thrift-store leftovers, and something else has taken their place. If the things we consume get that much say in who we are and how we act, is there really a difference between them and a deity?

The second way of looking at this piece is that it’s just a funny looking poop guy. I tend to stray towards the latter.
