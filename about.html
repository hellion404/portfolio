I’m Nate Langley, a studio art major at the University of Maryland, College Park, and a future art teacher. I’m in UMD’s Integrated Master’s Certification Program, so I’ll finish my B.A. in Studio Art in 2027 and my Master of Education in 2028. Outside the studio, I’m the graphics director at WMUC 90.5 FM, and I work with kids as an art teacher and childcare aide.

My practice centers on sculpture, painting, illustration, and graphic design, driven by a love for learning new techniques and constant experimentation. I’m often drawn to things that are loud, strange, or make me uncomfortable because I believe the best art comes from curiosity rather than playing it safe.

Off the clock I collect and read comics, paint Warhammer miniatures, and DJ a weekly radio show.
